# Charts Renderer

Charts are evidence, not decoration.

Choose the chart from the analytical question: comparison, trend, composition, distribution, relationship, ranking, flow. Do not force brand colors onto every series if distinction suffers.

Rules:
- include units/time range/source status;
- semantic colors remain consistent across artifacts;
- use direct labels where they reduce lookup;
- avoid 3D chart effects unless spatial meaning is real;
- axes/baselines must not distort the conclusion;
- provide accessible differentiation beyond color when needed;
- preserve exact data provenance.

QA must verify numbers, categories, sort order, axis ranges, labels, legend and intended takeaway.
