# Design Direction Library

This library is for divergence, not templates. Select by content, audience and Kernel; do not equate “current” with “correct”.

- Editorial Monument: strong type hierarchy, asymmetric editorial grid, restrained ornament.
- Quiet Editorial: warm/neutral surfaces, refined spacing, photography-led composition.
- Information Utility: rigorous navigation/tables/labels, one semantic accent, scan efficiency.
- Cinematic Information Architecture: spatial hierarchy, staged reveal, diagrams as architecture.
- Kinetic Type: typography as a dynamic geometric object; motion must aid meaning.
- Tactile Atelier: paper, print, grain and material depth with controlled craftsmanship.
- Generative Geometry: parameterized visual systems whose forms relate to content/data.
- Narrative Mini-Museum: chapters as distinct exhibits tied by one visual metaphor.
- Cultural Archive: source-aware regional/archival material with provenance and restraint.
- Imperfect Zine: collage, annotation and print artifacts with preserved reading order.
- Soft Playful 3D: few meaningful dimensional objects, not decorative 3D everywhere.
- Surreal Editorial: controlled reality distortion while factual content remains stable.

Anti-template check: if replacing the title with an unrelated topic leaves the design equally plausible, the visual subject is probably too generic.
