# Content Semantics

Before visual direction, translate source material into a medium-neutral content model. Do not confuse source order with final presentation order.

Minimum model:
- purpose and audience;
- key claim / desired outcome;
- facts, numbers and names with source status;
- information hierarchy;
- entities and relationships;
- narrative arc or decision path;
- required tables/charts/figures;
- legal, privacy, attribution and uncertainty constraints;
- content that must remain verbatim vs may be rewritten;
- density and reading/viewing context.

Keep three layers separate:
1. `source`: evidence and source locators;
2. `production`: internal structure, scene/section IDs, design notes;
3. `audience`: final visible/read-aloud content.

For cross-medium work, preserve semantics while changing expression. A DOCX chapter, PPT slide sequence and Web interaction can express the same model differently.
